The **Registration** tab of a zone shows what the registry says about its registered domain, next to
what DNZ serves: the registrar, the dates, every status with what it means, the nameservers and
DNSSEC at the registry compared with the zone's own, and the contacts the registry publishes.

<Note>
  A lookup on demand, for any zone, with [expiry monitoring](/dns/domain-expiry) on or off. Nothing
  runs in the background for it. While monitoring is on, its daily check keeps this fresh too.
</Note>

## Look it up

On the zone's page, **Registration** → **Look up**. Or from the terminal:

```bash
dnz zone info example.com
dnz zone info example.com --refresh   # ask again even if the last answer is under an hour old
```

An answer less than an hour old is reused; a refresh still asks the registry at most once a minute.

## What it shows

| Part | From the registry | Compared with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Registrar | Name, IANA registrar ID, website, abuse email and phone | – |
| Dates | Registered, last changed, expires | – |
| Statuses | Each status (`clientTransferProhibited`, `clientHold`, `redemptionPeriod`, …) explained in plain English; holds and deletion in red | – |
| Nameservers | The NS the registry delegates to | The zone's NS records; missing and extra ones highlighted |
| DNSSEC | Signed or not, and the DS records | The DS for the keys DNZ signs with: complete, DS missing, a DS that matches no key, or a DS left behind for an unsigned zone |
| Contacts | Registrant, admin, tech, billing | – |

Most registries withhold contact details for privacy; those show as **redacted**.

For a zone inside the registered domain (`lab.example.com` in `example.com`), the registry's
nameservers and DS are about `example.com`, so DNZ shows them without comparing.

## RDAP, then WHOIS

DNZ asks the registry over RDAP. For a top-level domain without RDAP (some country domains) it
falls back to **WHOIS** on TCP port 43, using the server IANA's `whois.iana.org` names, and reads the
usual fields from the answer, best effort. The tab says which one answered and, for WHOIS, shows the
original text.

## API

| Method | Path | Permission | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `GET` | `/api/zones/{id}/info` | `zones.read` | The last answer, compared with the zone as it is now (asks nobody) |
| `POST` | `/api/zones/{id}/info?refresh=true` | `zones.read` | Ask the registry; without `refresh`, an answer under an hour old is reused |

The answer holds `info` (the registry's data, with `source` `rdap` or `whois`), `statuses` (each with
`epp`, `meaning` and `level`), `nameservers` and `dnssec` (the comparisons, each with a `note`),
`checked_at`, `cached` and `error` (why the last lookup failed; `info` then keeps the answer before).
