---
title: "Sign in with Google"
description: "Link your DNZ account to your Google account once, then sign in with Google. Only linked accounts can, and the second factor stays."
---

People sign in to DNZ with their Google account instead of the password. Each person links their
own DNZ account to a Google account once; after that the sign-in page shows **Continue with
Google**. It's off until a superadmin sets up a Google OAuth client.

- **Only linked accounts sign in with Google.** An email address alone never matches an account.
- **The second factor stays.** With an authenticator app or passkey on, DNZ still asks for it after Google.
- **The password keeps working.** Google is a second way in, not a replacement.

## Set it up

You need to be a superadmin. Open **Settings → Integrations → Sign in with Google → Set up**: the
guide fills in the redirect URI for the address you type and has a copy button for each value.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Consent screen">
    In Google Cloud, open [Google Auth Platform](https://console.cloud.google.com/auth/overview). If the
    project has no consent screen yet, set one up: audience **Internal** for your Google Workspace
    organisation only, otherwise **External**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a client">
    [Clients → Create client](https://console.cloud.google.com/auth/clients/create): application type
    **Web application**, name `DNZ`. Under **Authorized redirect URIs**, add the address people open
    DNZ at plus `/api/auth/google/callback`:

    ```text
    https://dnz.example.com/api/auth/google/callback
    ```

    Opened at more than one address (a secondary, a second name)? Add one URI for each.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it into DNZ">
    **Create**, then paste the client ID (`….apps.googleusercontent.com`) and the client secret, and
    **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Google only sends the browser back to `https://` addresses (or `http://localhost`). Put DNZ behind
  HTTPS first if people open it over plain HTTP.
</Note>

**Only these Google domains** (optional) limits it to your Google Workspace domain, so personal
Gmail accounts can neither be linked nor sign in.

## Link your account

Open the user menu (top right) → **Account** → **Sign in with Google**. Confirm your password and
click **Link Google account**; Google asks which account to use and sends you back. The same place
shows the linked account, when it was last used, and **Unlink**.

A Google account can be linked to one DNZ account only. Linking asks for your password, so a stolen
session alone can't add a way in.

## Sign in

Click **Continue with Google** on the sign-in page. If your account has a second factor, DNZ asks
for it next. A deactivated account doesn't sign in, linked or not.

The [audit log](/admin/audit-log) records each sign-in as `user.login` with method `google`, refused
ones as `user.login_failed`, and links as `user.google_link` / `user.google_unlink`.

## Manage links

Superadmins see a **Google** badge on linked accounts under **Settings → Users**, and
**Unlink Google** in each one's menu.

## Secondaries

A DNZ secondary copies the Google client and everyone's links from its primary, so the same people
sign in with Google on both. Add the secondary's address to the client's redirect URIs in Google
Cloud too. Copied accounts link and unlink on the primary.

## From the CLI

```bash
dnz settings google-sign-in                     # on or off, the client, how many accounts are linked
DNZ_GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET=GOCSPX-… dnz settings google-sign-in set \
  --client-id 1234-abc.apps.googleusercontent.com --domain example.com
dnz settings google-sign-in set --client-id … --any-domain   # drop the domain limit
dnz settings google-sign-in off                 # links stay, for when it is back on
dnz user list                                   # the GOOGLE column
dnz user unlink-google budi
```

Linking itself happens in the dashboard: it needs a browser.
