title: "Topology map"
description: "Your whole DNS setup on one interactive canvas, with live health drawn on it."
---

**Topology** in the menu draws everything DNZ knows on one canvas:

- **Divisions** with their members, roles and API tokens.
- **Zones** with records by type, DNSSEC and Cloudflare connections.
- **Caddies and Traefiks** of [Sites in DNS](/integrations/caddy), linked to the zones their sites' records are in.
- **Client networks, views and blocklists**, and **client groups** (the named devices that some
  lists and rules are limited to), linked to the filtering they have their own rules in.
- **Steered records**: an **Origin** node per steered A/AAAA address, with its place (GeoDNS) or
  weight, and its health check state (*healthy* or *down*, with the error). See
  [GeoDNS and health checks](/dns/geodns).
- **The query path**: clients → filtering → the cache → forwarding rules → upstreams. The filtering
  node shows safe search and how many lists or rules are only for some clients or hours; the cache
  node shows the per-client rate limit and [RRL](/dns/rate-limiting).
- **Response policy zones**: each subscribed **RPZ** zone feeds into filtering (checked first), with
  its rule count, policy and last error; a **Served as RPZ** node shows the zone resolvers transfer
  DNZ's lists from. See [Response policy zones](/dns/rpz).
- **Replication**: primaries, secondaries and catalog zones.
- **Cluster nodes**: the other DNZ servers on the same database, with their state (up, behind or
  down) and the leader. See [Cluster](/deploy/cluster).
- **Proxies**: proxied names, origins and certificates.
- **Notification providers**.
- **Backup targets**, red while their backups fail.

Live state is drawn on the nodes and edges: upstream health and outages, query counts, blocked
queries, origin health checks, RPZ errors and certificate expiry.

## Using the map

- **Filter** by division or by layer, and **search** for a node.
- **Drag** nodes around. Positions are remembered.
- **Click** a node to open its page.
- **Export** the map as PNG or SVG.

Everyone sees only what their permissions allow. A division member sees their divisions; the
server-wide parts (query path, forwarding, replication, proxies, notifications) are for superadmins.

## In the terminal

`dnz topology` prints the same as an outline:

```bash
dnz topology
dnz topology --division it
dnz --json topology
```

See [CLI: monitoring](/cli/monitoring).
