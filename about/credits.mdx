DNZ stands on the work of many open-source projects. Thank you to everyone who builds and maintains
them. Each one keeps its own license, listed next to it.

### Blocklists and threat feeds

DNZ downloads these only when you turn them on under [Filtering](/dns/filtering) or
[Threats](/dns/threats). They belong to their maintainers and keep their own licenses.

- [HaGeZi DNS blocklists](https://github.com/hagezi/dns-blocklists)
- [StevenBlack hosts](https://github.com/StevenBlack/hosts)
- [OISD](https://oisd.nl/)
- [AdGuard DNS filter](https://github.com/AdguardTeam/AdGuardSDNSFilter)
- [The Block List Project](https://github.com/blocklistproject/Lists)
- [abuse.ch URLhaus](https://urlhaus.abuse.ch/) and [ThreatFox](https://threatfox.abuse.ch/)

<Note>
  This page lists the projects DNZ uses directly. Each of them pulls in more crates and packages,
  and their licenses come with them. `cargo tree` in the source and `web/package.json` show the full
  list.
</Note>
